# Remaining direct checks

This worksheet contains procedures, not participant results or learning-log answers.

## SC-004: brief repair entry

Use the V1 demo and a teammate who has not rehearsed the exact sequence. Give them a one-line next action to record on an open repair. Start timing when they begin the task and stop when the stamped entry is visible. Record taps, typed content, errors, elapsed seconds and assistance. The criterion is under 30 seconds with at most one short line typed. Repeat with gloves/noise only if the group can create an appropriate safe classroom simulation; no real machine interaction is needed.

## SC-005: independent incoming-shift comprehension

Have a teammate who did not create the repair entry open the machine view. Ask them to identify the next action and the last person who worked on the repair without asking the author. Record the answer and time, then ask them to acknowledge with the correct demo identity. Distinguish reading comprehension from successful button operation. The target is under 30 seconds and one acknowledgment action.

## Browser photo path

On an open repair, choose a small synthetic/non-personal PNG/JPEG and save a photo-only entry. Confirm it is visible with automatic author/time, and inspect the image. Try empty text/no image and an unsupported/oversize file. Record actual outcomes. Use a test image rather than unrelated personal documents.
