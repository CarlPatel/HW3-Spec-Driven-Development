Interviewer: Can you tell me about the plants you have at home and how you currently take
care of them?

Stakeholder: My roommate and I have a few plants around our apartment. We both take care
of them, but neither of us is particularly good at remembering when they need attention. Right
now, we have a handwritten chart on our fridge where we write down when each plant was last
watered.

Interviewer: Is the chart working for you?

Stakeholder: Not really. Sometimes one of us waters a plant but forgets to update the chart.
Other times, we both assume the other person has watered it, and it ends up not getting
watered for several days. We've also accidentally watered the same plant twice.

Interviewer: What would make tracking watering easier?

Stakeholder: I'd like to see all our plants in one place, when each was last watered, and when it
needs to be watered next. Ideally, either of us should be able to update it whenever we water
something.

Interviewer: Do all your plants need watering at the same frequency?

Stakeholder: No, that's another problem. Some need water more frequently than others. I'd like
to set a watering schedule for each plant rather than have the same schedule for everything.

Interviewer: Apart from watering, is there anything else you struggle with?

Stakeholder: Sunlight is a big one. We sometimes move plants outside or near the balcony to
get some sun, but then forget to bring them back inside. It would be helpful to track when we
moved a plant outside and when we should bring it back.

Interviewer: How would you decide when a plant needs to be brought back inside?

Stakeholder: I think we could just specify how long we want to leave it outside. Different plants
might need different amounts of time, so I'd rather set that ourselves.

Interviewer: How would you like the application to remind you about these things?

Stakeholder: A dashboard showing what needs attention would be useful. Maybe something
that clearly shows which plants need watering and which ones are still outside. Notifications
would be nice too, but I don't think we need those immediately.

Interviewer: Since both you and your roommate take care of the plants, how should that work?
Stakeholder: We should both be able to see the same information and update it. If my
roommate waters a plant, I should be able to see that it was watered so I don't do it again. It
might also be useful to know who last took care of a plant.

Interviewer: What happens if you forget to water a plant on its scheduled day?

Stakeholder: It should probably show that it's overdue until someone waters it. I wouldn't want it
to automatically mark anything as done.

Interviewer: Would you want the application to automatically determine how much water or
sunlight each plant needs?

Stakeholder: Not for now. We can enter that information ourselves. I mostly want something
that helps us stay organized rather than having to look at the fridge chart every day.

Interviewer: What's the most important thing you'd want from the first version?

Stakeholder: Just being able to add our plants, keep track of watering and sunlight, and see
what needs attention. If we can get those things working, it would already be a big improvement.
